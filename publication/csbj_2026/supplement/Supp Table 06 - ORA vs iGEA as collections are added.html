Supplementary Table 6

ORA and iGEA networks as gene-set collections are added

Collections are added in a fixed order: the three Gene Ontology collections, then Reactome, KEGG Legacy and KEGG MEDICUS, WikiPathways, BioCarta and Pathway Interaction Database, and Hallmark (10 collections). ORA: hypergeometric test (upper tail) on the full gene list; gene sets restricted to the background and to 10-600 genes (the same sets as iGEA); Benjamini-Hochberg FDR within each collection; terms with FDR < 0.05 and at least 3 input genes, each linked to all its input genes. "ORA (all set sizes)": the same without the 10-600 filter, as ORA is usually run. iGEA: terms kept (empirical q < 0.10, 10,000 random gene lists) in the 10-collection analysis, restricted to the collections of each row; each gene is assigned to at most one term per collection. (For HIV with GO alone, the separately tested GO network of Figure 2 and Figure 3B has 26 terms and 138 genes; the GO rows below are the GO terms of the 10-collection analysis, 27 terms and 144 genes.) Edges: gene-term links. Max gene degree: largest number of terms linked to one gene. Communities and modularity: Leiden (igraph, modularity) on the term network, terms linked by shared genes, best of 100 seeds (the procedure of the iGEA software); modularity describes how cleanly the terms separate into communities (descriptive, not a test).
